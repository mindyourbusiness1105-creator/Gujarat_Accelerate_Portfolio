About NEN

National Entrepreneurship Network (NEN) is an independent, not-for-profit organization advancing entrepreneurship across India. Through entrepreneurship education, AI-enabled platforms, business acceleration and ecosystem partnerships, we empower aspiring entrepreneurs, startups, SMEs and institutions to build stronger businesses, create sustainable jobs and contribute to India’s economic growth.
